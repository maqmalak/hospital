# Accounts & finance

## Finance overview
**Finance overview**: today's and this month's revenue by department, collections by mode, receivables (patients, panels, insurers), payables and cash position.

## Expenses
**Expenses**: record an expense with a bill photo; approval runs by amount. **Recurring** tab: rent, salaries of contractors, internet — drafted on their date into the same approval flow.

## Panels, insurance and welfare
- **Panels & insurers**: companies and insurers with rates and credit limits.
- **Insurance pre-auth**: approvals before admission.
- **Insurance claims**: claims from final bills, submission and settlement.
- **Welfare & zakat**: approved patients' discounts charged to the welfare fund.

## Doctors
**Doctor share**: each doctor's share by fee and procedure, earned when the patient pays; pay doctors as suppliers.

## Payments and tax
- **Online card payments**: gateway payments and their settlement.
- **FBR reporting**: invoices reported to FBR (POS integration).

## Buying and assets
**Supplier invoices** are paid from ERPNext; **Fixed assets** depreciate monthly. See the *Store, buying & assets* manual.

## Reports
**Reports**: OPD, lab, pharmacy, IPD, cash and doctor reports; ERPNext's P&L, balance sheet and stock reports through **Desk**.
