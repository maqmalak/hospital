# Administration

## Users & roles
**Users & roles**: create staff users, give roles (receptionist, doctor, nurse, lab user, pathologist, pharmacy roles, store keeper, cash supervisor, accountant), lock each user to the branch and to counters. **Page access** tab: which roles may open which screen.

## Clinic settings
**Clinic settings**: modules on / off (lab, pharmacy, wards, emergency, online visits), OPD rules (nurse takes vitals, follow-up days), cash rules (blind count, tolerance, discount limits), printing (printer map, headers), messaging (WhatsApp templates, test mode).

## Masters
**Doctors** (fees, schedules, share), **Clinical masters** (medicines, allergies, ICD-10), **Test templates**, wards and beds.

## Watching the system
- **Audit log**: who viewed or changed patient records.
- **Dashboard**: the day at a glance.
- **Patient feedback**: ratings after OPD and discharge.
- **Display devices**: waiting-area TVs and ward kiosks.
- Backups run every hour (see OPS.md); a health check reports scheduler, failed jobs and the last backup.

## Records
**Merge patients** joins duplicate records (history, bills and results move to one MR).
