# Pharmacy

## Pharmacy POS
1. **Pharmacy POS**: open your shift. Keys: F4 load prescription, F7 return, F8 drafts, F10 invoices, F11 shift summary.
2. **Load prescription**: search by patient, MR, mobile or Rx number. Lines arrive with quantities; stock and batches are picked FEFO (first expiry first).
3. Customer: walk-in, patient or credit (on account) for approved customers.
4. Discount in % or Rs within your limit; above it the supervisor approves.
5. **Pay now** (split tender, transaction ID for card / wallets) or **Pay at handover**.
6. If the internet drops, cash / card sales without discount or prescription items are queued and posted on reconnect.

## Pipeline
- **Pharmacy pick** (finder): pick each line, scan the batch.
- **Pharmacy review** (pharmacist): check the batch, expiry and prescription; controlled drugs are logged.
- **Pharmacy handover** (dispenser): hand over and take payment if it is pay-at-handover.
- **Pharmacy pipeline**: every ticket and where it is.

## Returns
Return by line from the invoice (F7); the refund comes from your shift and the stock goes back to its batch.
