# Laboratory

## Two ways a patient reaches the lab
- **Sent by reception or the doctor**: the order is already made and paid at the reception counter.
- **Walk-in at the lab**: register the patient here (**Patient registration**), make the order in **Lab orders** → **Save & send to reception**. The patient pays at the **Reception counter**; the receipt shows the test.

## Paid before sampling
**Lab orders** and **Lab samples** show **Paid** / **Unpaid** on every order. A sample can only be collected after payment (unless the order is on credit — panel, insurance, admitted patient).

## Samples
1. **Lab samples** → *Waiting*: paid orders ready to collect. **Collect** prints the barcode label.
2. **Receive** in the lab; **Reject** with a reason (clotted, haemolysed) — the patient is asked for a new sample.

## Results
1. **Lab results**: enter values; the normal range and flag (High / Low / Critical) show beside each value. Previous results are shown.
2. A critical value must be phoned and recorded (who was told) before verification.
3. Text and scanned / handwritten reports can be attached.

## Verification (pathologist)
**Lab verification**: results waiting, critical first. Add an interpretation and **Verify & sign**, or **Send back** with a reason. Lab staff can view this queue; only the pathologist signs.

## Reports
The verified report prints with the logo, QR code, signature, stamp and history. It can be sent on WhatsApp; the QR proves it is genuine.

## Outsourced tests and templates
- **Outsourced tests**: dispatch samples to a reference lab (courier), record the result and attach their report.
- **Test templates**: parameters, units, ranges by age and gender, prices.

## Shift
The lab counter has its own cashier shift for the rare case lab staff take cash; **Close shift** at the end.
