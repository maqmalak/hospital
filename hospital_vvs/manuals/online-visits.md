# Online visits

## How a patient books
The public page /hms/online-visit takes the patient's details, complaint and preferred doctor; payment by JazzCash, Easypaisa or card.

## Staff
1. **Online visits**: new requests; verify the payment (or collect it at the **Reception counter**), assign the doctor and time.
2. The patient gets a WhatsApp message with the video link.
3. The doctor joins, writes the prescription in the **Prescription workspace**, and it is sent to the patient.

## Reports
**Online visit report**: visits, fees and doctors by day.
