# Doctor

## Your queue
1. **Prescription workspace** shows your pending patients: *At vitals* (with the nurse) and *Ready* (vitals done). Urgent and elderly patients come first.
2. **Call next** or click a patient. The nurse's vitals, chief complaint and the last visits are on screen.

## Writing a prescription
1. **Medicine**: type a brand or generic; the drop-down shows strength and stock. Dose, frequency (1+0+1, BD, TDS…) and duration fill the quantity automatically (tablets, syrup bottles, packs).
2. Safety alerts appear as you add: allergy, duplicate generic, pregnancy and dose limits. Override with a reason when you must.
3. **Diagnosis**: one-click chips for common diagnoses, or search ICD-10.
4. **Lab tests**: tick the tests; a lab order is created and billed at reception.
5. Advice, follow-up date and Urdu instructions print on the prescription.
6. **Sign & print**: a signed prescription can no longer be edited. Reprint from **Patient history** at any time.

## History
**Patient history**: every visit, vitals trend, prescriptions, lab results and admissions on one screen.

## Wards and emergency
- **Admissions** → open a patient: ward rounds, orders (medicines, labs, diet, procedures), progress notes.
- Medicines ordered for inpatients go to the **Drug round** (MAR) for the nurses.
- **Emergency**: see the triage board, take a patient, then admit, send home or refer.
- **Discharge**: on the admission, fill the discharge summary (diagnosis, treatment, condition, medicines at home, follow-up) and click **Sign summary**; the bill can only be finalised after you sign.

## Commission
**My commission** shows your share of fees and procedures, earned when the patient pays.
