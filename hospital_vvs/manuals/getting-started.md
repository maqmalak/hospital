# Getting started

## Signing in
1. Open /hms/login on any counter PC, ward tablet or your phone. Sign in with your work email and password.
2. You land on the **Desktop**: one tile for every screen your role may open. Tiles you are not allowed to use are not shown.
3. Each user may be signed in on up to 5 devices. Sign out from the top bar on shared PCs.

## The top bar
- **Branch** shows where you work (Lahore Main). Your records and bills are kept to that branch.
- **English / اردو** switches the whole screen and the patient prints to Urdu. Patient names have an Urdu field that fills itself as you type the English name; correct a word once and every screen learns it.
- **Moon / sun** switches dark mode.
- **Desk** opens ERPNext (accounts and stock reports) for staff who need it. Daily work never needs it.

## Finding a patient
Every patient search accepts the **MR number**, mobile number, CNIC, name or a scan of the patient card. Duplicate checks run when you register: the same name, age and phone is flagged before a second record is made.

## The patient journey
A typical OPD patient moves: **Appointments** (online or phone) → **Patient registration** → **OPD slip** and token → **OPD vitals** (nurse) → **Prescription workspace** (doctor) → **Lab orders** / **Pharmacy POS** → home. An inpatient continues: **Admissions** → **Drug round** → **IPD bill** → discharge. Watch the *Full patient journey* video for the whole path.
