# Wards & emergency

## Emergency
1. **Emergency** → **Register**: an existing patient or an unknown one (gender and approximate age; identified later). Arrival mode (Rescue 1122, walk-in), brought by, medico-legal (MLC number, police station).
2. **Triage** red / yellow / green with vitals. The board sorts by triage and waiting time.
3. Assign a doctor and an ER bay; order labs and medicines.
4. **Disposition**: *Admitted* (choose bed and doctor — every ER charge moves to the admission), *Discharged home* (ER bill in the reception shift), *Referred out*, *LAMA* (signed form) or *Expired*.

## Admission
1. **Admissions** → **Admit**: patient, bed (from the bed map), admitting doctor, reason, provisional diagnosis, planned days, payer (patient, panel, insurance with pre-auth), package.
2. Take the **advance**; the bedside QR link lets the family see updates and call the nurse.
3. Bed charges count by the midnight census; nursing, procedures, medicines and labs add to the running bill.

## Ward care
Vitals chart, nursing and progress notes, ward rounds, medicine orders (charted on the **Drug round**), diet orders (**Diet list**), procedures with consent, lab orders. Transfer to another bed from the admission.

## Discharge
1. The doctor fills the discharge summary on the admission and clicks **Sign summary** (discharge type: normal, LAMA, referred, expired).
2. Clearance: summary signed, no lab pending, every due dose charted, no procedure open.
3. **IPD bill** → **Final bill** (discount with approval), then take the settlement — or refund extra advance.
4. **Complete discharge**: the bed goes to *Cleaning*, the bedside link stops, and the summary prints.
