# Nurse

## OPD vitals
1. **OPD vitals** lists patients waiting for vitals (urgent and elderly first) with their last readings.
2. Click the patient, enter BP, pulse, temperature, SpO₂, RBS, weight and height (BMI is worked out). Impossible readings are refused.
3. Add the chief complaint and set the priority, then **Save & send to the doctor**: the token moves to *Waiting* on the **Physician queue**.

## Ward work
1. **Wards & beds**: the bed map — Available, Occupied, Cleaning, Reserved, Maintenance. Mark a bed clean when housekeeping is done.
2. **Admissions** → open the patient: chart vitals, add nursing notes, see orders.
3. **Drug round**: doses due in the next hours by ward. Chart each dose *Administered*, *Held* or *Refused* with a reason. Overdue doses are red.
4. **Diet list**: today's diets per patient for the kitchen.
5. **Nursing stations**: patient calls from the bedside link; response times are measured.

## Emergency
**Emergency**: triage each arrival red / yellow / green with vitals; red goes to a bay and a doctor at once.

## Discharge
When the doctor signs the discharge summary, chart every due dose (clearance checks it); the desk then makes the final bill and completes the discharge.

## Roster
**Nurse roster** shows who is on duty per ward and shift; the head nurse approves gated doses.
