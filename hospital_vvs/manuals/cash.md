# Cash & shifts

## Opening a shift
**Cashier shift** → your counter → opening float by mode (cash float usually) → **Open shift**. Every receipt, refund and IPD payment you take belongs to that shift.

## During the shift
- **Pay in / Pay out**: petty cash in or out with a reason (printer paper, change).
- **Cash drop**: send excess cash to the safe; the supervisor confirms it.
- **Refund**: from the bills list of the shift.
- The card **By payment mode** shows opening, collections, pay in / out, drops and expected per mode.

## Closing
1. **Close shift**: count the cash by denomination (notes and coins).
2. Card, JazzCash, Easypaisa and bank are **filled in from the system** — change them only if the terminal or wallet slip differs (the box turns amber; **Reset** puts the system figure back).
3. **Submit count**: balanced or within the tolerance closes at once and posts the POS closing; a bigger difference waits for the supervisor.

## Supervisor
- **Cash approvals**: approve a short / excess (posted to Cash Short / Excess) or ask for a recount; confirm cash drops.
- **Discount approvals**: discounts above a cashier's limit.
- **Cash reports**: shifts by counter and user, differences, collections by mode.
