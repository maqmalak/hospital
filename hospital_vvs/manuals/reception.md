# Reception & front desk

## Start of the day
1. **Cashier shift** → choose your counter (Reception 1 or 2) → enter the opening float → **Open shift**. No money can be taken outside a shift.
2. Check **Appointments** for today's bookings (reception, website and WhatsApp).

## Registering a patient
1. **Patient registration**: search first (MR, mobile, CNIC, name). Only register when the patient is not found.
2. Enter the name (Urdu fills itself), gender, age or date of birth, mobile, guardian (S/O, D/O, W/O) and city. CNIC is optional and shown masked.
3. **Register & OPD slip** saves the patient (MR number) and opens the OPD slip. **Patient card** prints a card with a QR code.

## OPD slip and token
1. **OPD slip**: pick the doctor; the fee comes from the doctor's fee schedule (follow-up and welfare rates apply automatically).
2. **Save & print**: fee receipt and token. The token goes to the nurse for vitals first, then to the doctor (**Physician queue**).

## Online and phone appointments
1. **Appointments** lists bookings by doctor and time; website bookings arrive with the patient's details and payment status.
2. When the patient arrives, click **Arrived** on the booking, then **OPD slip**: the slip and token are issued for the booked doctor.
3. **WhatsApp inbox** shows chats handed over to reception by the booking bot.

## Reception counter (one cart)
**Reception counter** puts everything a patient owes in one cart: OPD fee, services (ECG, dressing), **lab tests sent from the lab** ("From the lab" tab), IPD advances and old dues.
1. Find the patient; pending lab orders and dues appear by themselves.
2. Discount: type it in **%** or **Rs**; within your limit it applies at once, above it the supervisor approves (**Discount approvals**).
3. Take payment: cash, card, JazzCash, Easypaisa or bank transfer — split if needed. Print the receipt. Lab orders show **Paid** in the lab at once.

## Admissions and emergency
- **Admissions** → **Admit**: patient, bed, doctor, reason, advance. The bedside QR link is printed for the family.
- **Emergency**: register an arrival (unknown patients get a temporary record), the nurse triages.
- **IPD bill**: running bill, advances, final bill and settlement at discharge.

## End of the day
**Close shift**: count the notes by denomination. Card, JazzCash and other modes are filled in from the system — change them only if the slip differs. A difference above the tolerance waits for the cash supervisor.
