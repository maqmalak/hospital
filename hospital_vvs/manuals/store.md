# Store, buying & assets

## Medicines and stock
- **Medicine master**: brand, generic, strength, pack size, prescription-only, controlled, reorder level.
- **Receive stock**: receive against a purchase order with batch and expiry.
- **Store transfers**: main store → pharmacy, IPD dispensing, lab.
- **Cycle count**: count a shelf; differences post as stock adjustments.
- **Expiry & returns**: near-expiry list, quarantine and return to the supplier.

## Buying
1. **Material requests**: wards, lab and pharmacy ask for items; you approve.
2. **Requests for quotation** to several suppliers; their **Supplier quotations** go on the comparative statement.
3. **Purchase orders**: to a supplier (Pakistani pharmaceutical distributors are set up) with rates, taxes and delivery date.
4. **Purchase receipts**: goods received into the right store; medicines with batch and expiry. Fixed-asset items need a location.
5. **Supplier invoices**: the bill from the supplier against the receipt; accounts pays it (**Payment entries**).
6. **Landed costs**: freight and duty added to the cost of received goods.
7. **Suppliers** and supplier groups: contact, NTN / STRN, payment terms, balance.

Every buying document shows its items, taxes, ledger, activity and linked documents, and the **Actions** menu makes the next one (order → receipt → invoice → payment).

## Fixed assets
**Fixed assets**: machinery and infrastructure (X-ray, ultrasound, ventilators, generators). Each asset has a location, custodian, useful life and depreciation; activate it to start depreciation. Movements and repairs are recorded.

## Consumption
**Consumption**: consumables issued to wards, OT and the lab are charged to the department's expense account.
