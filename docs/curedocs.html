OPD Management
Complete outpatient department workflow — registration to prescription

👤 Patient Registration

Registration Methods
  • Walk-in registration at reception desk
  • Online self-registration via patient portal
  • Auto-creates ERPNext Customer on registration
  • Unique patient ID generated automatically
  • Profile: name, phone, age, gender, allergies
  • Emergency contacts & family members
  • Insurance & panel details captured
  • Multi-language: English, Urdu, Arabic names
Patient Profile
  • Basic info: name, phone, age, gender
  • Allergies (custom allergy list)
  • Emergency contacts
  • Family members (linked visits)
  • Insurance provider & policy number
  • Visit & prescription history
  • Panel / corporate details

👨‍⚕️ Doctor Setup

Doctor Configuration
  • Doctor name in English, Urdu, Arabic
  • Specialization (Cardiology, Pediatrics, etc.)
  • Default consultation fee per doctor
  • Color tag for queue identification
  • Linked user account for portal login
  • Availability schedule (days & time windows)
  • Time slot management (auto-generated)
  • Doctor leave periods (blocks slot booking)
  • License & qualification display on prescription
  • Doctor photo on prescription header

📅 Appointments & Time Slot Management

Appointment Features
  • Online booking via patient portal (/book-appointment)
  • Walk-in via queue or direct OPD slip
  • Status: Pending → Confirmed → Completed / Rejected
  • Payment channels configurable (cash, card, online)
  • Payment proof screenshot upload
  • Refund management for cancellations
  • Booking reference generated for patient
Time Slot Management
  • Auto-generate slots days ahead (configurable window)
  • Slot blocking by admin or doctor
  • Auto-release no-show slots after N minutes
  • Blocked date management (clinic holidays)
  • Walk-in handling mode configurable
  • Time display: 12h or 24h format
  • Per-doctor slot configuration override

🚶 Physician Queue

Live Patient Queue Portal: /physician-queue
  • Real-time queue visible to all clinic staff
  • Auto-add to queue on consultation payment
  • Queue organized per doctor
  • Token number displayed on OPD slip
  • Shows chief complaint & patient name
  • Doctor marks patient as called / done
  • Waiting count visible at reception
  • Can be shown on a waiting room screen
  • Follow-up patients flagged separately
  • Emergency / priority override available

🧾 OPD Slip & Billing

OPD Slip Fields
  • Patient, doctor, date & time
  • Purpose: Consultation / Follow-up / Procedure
  • Chief complaint & clinical notes
  • Vitals: BP, pulse, temp, weight, SpO₂
  • Line items with service codes & fees
  • Discount & payment method
  • Cash change calculation
Billing Options
  • Auto-invoice on slip creation (configurable)
  • Skip invoice for zero-fee patients
  • POS Profile for OPD (configurable)
  • Follow-up free within configurable days
  • Multiple payment modes: Cash, Card, Online
  • Auto-add to physician queue after payment
  • Refund via credit note

📋 Prescription Writing

💡
Doctor Portal (/pw-prescription): Full-featured prescription writing interface designed for clinical speed — voice control, auto-complete medicines with structured dosing, and instant printing.
Prescription Components
  • Medicines — name, dose, frequency, duration, instructions
  • Diagnoses — categorized ICD-supported diagnosis list
  • Lab Tests — ordered directly from prescription
  • Instructions — free-text clinical notes
  • Examination Notes — clinical findings
  • Follow-up date — auto-calculated from revisit days
  • Round number for IPD ward round prescriptions
Prescription Features
  • Voice control for hands-free entry
  • Generic name display toggle
  • Simple vs detailed dosing format
  • Header / footer / stamp configurable per clinic
  • Print via browser or thermal printer
  • Multi-language prescription output
  • Auto-create medicine invoice from prescription
  • Show / hide license on printed prescription

📁 Patient History

Patient History Portal Portal: /pw-history
  • Full visit timeline with dates and attending doctors
  • All previous prescriptions with medicine details
  • Diagnosis history across all visits
  • Vitals history chart (configurable entry count)
  • Lab test results history
  • IPD admission history
  • Payment and invoice history

📝 Ward Rounds & Progress Notes

IPD Prescription (Ward Round)
  • Linked to bed allotment
  • Round number tracked per admission
  • Full prescription: medicines, diagnoses, tests
  • Configurable note types per clinic
  • Progress notes with timestamps
  • Attending doctor per round
IPD Portal Portal: /ward
  • Live bed map showing ward occupancy
  • Per-patient timeline view
  • Quick prescription from bed card
  • IPD report generation
  • IPD slip printing
  • Re-admission tracking & history

💉 MAR – Medication Administration Record

  • Records every medicine administration event
  • Nurse marks medicine administered with timestamp
  • Two MAR modes: Simple or Detailed
  • Auto-deduct stock from IPD dispensing warehouse
  • Allow negative stock on MAR issue (configurable)
  • Bill administered medicines to patient account
  • MAR billing trigger: on administration or at discharge
  • Stock deduction timing: immediate or discharge
  • Oxygen & consumables recording
  • MAR medicines gate: approve before administration

🍽️ Diet Orders

  • Doctor orders diet type per patient per day
  • Types: Regular, Soft, Liquid, Diabetic, etc. (configurable)
  • Diet billing modes: per-day flat charge or per-meal
  • Configurable diet charge and billing item
  • Diet charges aggregated into IPD bill at discharge

🔧 Procedures

Procedure Scheduling
  • IPD procedure scheduling from admission record
  • Procedure team members logged
  • Procedure room types assigned
  • Invoice trigger: on schedule or on completion
  • Auto-submit procedure invoice option
Billing
  • Procedures billed via IPD billing gate
  • Ad-hoc services gate for unplanned services
  • Package billing: procedures included in package price
  • Auto-aggregate unpaid charges at discharge
  • Insurance claim support for procedures

Charge Types Included in IPD Invoice

  • Daily bed rate
  • ICU / Special bed surcharge
  • Room type differential

Clinical

  • MAR medicines administered
  • Procedures performed
  • Lab orders from IPD
  • Consumables used

Support

  • Per-shift nursing charges
  • Diet & meals
  • Oxygen charges
  • Ad-hoc services
  • Service Packages — bundle bed + procedures + nursing at fixed price
  • Insurance / TPA Billing — link to provider, policy, claim number

🚪 Discharge Process

1
Discharge Summary
Doctor writes final diagnosis, treatment summary, discharge medicines, and follow-up plan.
2
Patient Clearance
All pending charges reviewed. Insurance approval obtained. Final invoice generated from aggregated charges.
3
Consent & Documentation
Consent forms tracked (configurable mode). Discharge notes and instructions given to patient.
4
Bed Release
Bed status updated to Available. Allotment record closed with actual release date. Re-admission window starts.

ERPNext Integration
How CureDesk connects to the ERPNext accounting and inventory backbone
Accounts Integration
  • All patient invoices → ERPNext Sales Invoice via POS
  • OPD, Lab, Medicine, General — separate POS Profiles
  • Income accounts configurable per billing type
  • Cost centers mapped to departments
  • AR aging & collection via ERPNext
  • Expense entries → ERPNext Journal Entry
  • Purchase invoices → ERPNext Accounts Payable
  • Full P&L and Balance Sheet in ERPNext
Inventory Integration
  • Medicines managed as ERPNext Items
  • Stock deducted from source warehouse on medicine invoice
  • IPD dispensing warehouse separate from retail
  • Material issues for IPD internal consumption
  • Negative stock allowed option
  • Stock valuation via ERPNext (FIFO / Moving Average)
  • Purchase receipts via ERPNext PO → Receipt flow
Patient as ERPNext Customer
  • Each patient is auto-created as an ERPNext Customer on registration
  • All invoices linked to customer — full AR visibility
  • Panel / corporate patients use corporate customer groups for pricing
  • Insurance / TPA patients linked to insurance provider customer
  • Outstanding balance visible from customer record in ERPNext
✅
No double-entry needed: Clinical staff use CureDesk portal pages exclusively. ERPNext handles accounting and inventory in the background automatically — no ERPNext knowledge required from clinic staff.
