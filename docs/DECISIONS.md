| D-28 | 2026-11-09 | **IPD billing**: every billable thing during a stay is an **IPD Charge** row (medicines given, labs, procedures, diet, oxygen, consumables, extra services, ER charges carried over). Bed, ICU surcharge and nursing are computed from the bed history while the patient is in, and written as charges when the final bill is made. The final bill is **one Sales Invoice** on the IPD Slip POS Profile (credit, not POS) for the patient share, plus a payer invoice for the panel / insurance share. | One running bill a desk can show at any time; one invoice per stay, matching the spec's aggregated final invoice. |
| D-29 | 2026-11-09 | **Bed days = midnight census**: a day is charged to the bed the patient was in at midnight; a stay with no midnight is charged `min_bed_days` (default 1) on the admission bed. **Nursing** is charged per started shift (shift start times in Clinic Settings, default 08:00 / 14:00 / 20:00), to the bed the patient was in when the shift began. | Common practice in Pakistani hospitals; transfers never double-charge a day. Change `min_bed_days` or the shift times if the hospital counts differently. |
| D-30 | 2026-11-09 | **Advances and settlement are Payment Entries** tagged with the admission and the collector's cashier shift (`hv_shift`). The shift's expected cash includes them (refunds paid out subtract). Only that admission's advances are allocated on its final invoice. | ERPNext's standard advance flow; the cash count still balances. |
| D-31 | 2026-11-09 | **Packages** cover chosen charge types (optionally up to a limit per type); bed days beyond the package's days stay billable. **Payer share** = coverage % of the bill after package and discount, capped at the approved pre-auth amount. Insurance admissions need an approved, unexpired pre-auth (setting). Pre-auth is a minimal record now; claims come in Phase 6. | Spec 06 / 07; keeps Phase 6 free to add claims on the same record. |
| D-32 | 2026-11-09 | **Unknown ER patients** get a Patient with `temporary_record` and a `TMP-<branch>-` MR. | D-33 | 2026-11-09 | **Nurse roster uses HRMS** (installed on this site): Shift Type per nursing shift and a Shift Assignment per nurse, day and ward (custom fields `hv_ward`, `hv_is_head_nurse`). | D-34 | 2026-11-09 | Wards keep floor and room as fields on Ward / Hospital Bed instead of separate **Floor** and **Room** DocTypes; ICU is the ward type plus an ICU surcharge per day on the ward. **Ad-hoc Service Charge** and **Consumable Usage** are IPD Charge types rather than their own DocTypes. **Intake/output** are fields on Vitals Chart. | Fewer screens and one bill ledger; nothing in the workflows needs the extra DocTypes. Ask if you want them split out. |
